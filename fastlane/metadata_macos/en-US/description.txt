Onlynote brings simple notes, checklists and everyday plans to your Mac. Write with the feel of paper, keep useful details together, and find them when you need them.

WRITE AND ORGANIZE
• Capture ideas with simple formatting, a writing focus mode, undo and redo, automatic saving, and note history.
• Organize notes with folders, tags, pins, saved search views, Archive and Trash.
• Start from shopping, packing, daily-plan and meeting templates, or save your own templates.
• Add photos from your Mac and recognize their text on-device. Search can find text in your photos, too.
• Keep a daily note and revisit it through the calendar.
• Set reminders that take you back to the right note.
• Copy note text or share notes as an image, PDF or Markdown.

MADE FOR MAC
Use native menus, Command-key shortcuts and right-click note actions in a resizable window. Choose stationery styles, from classic paper and sticky notes to ruled notebooks and grid paper.

NATIVE DESKTOP WIDGETS
On macOS 14 or later, keep an overview of your library or a selected note or checklist on your desktop or in Notification Center. Check off checklist items in the widget and click a note to open it in Onlynote.

YOUR LOCAL LIBRARY
No account or sign-in is required. Each device has its own local library. Transfer notes, photos and recordings between Mac and iPhone by exporting a .onlynote backup and restoring it on the other device.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The Mac app is ad-free. The free plan includes up to 10 notes, checklists, one-time reminders, up to 3 folders and 3 saved templates, and default card styling.

A one-time Premium purchase unlocks unlimited notes, folders and saved templates, repeating reminders, voice memos, individual card styles, and Premium note themes. No subscription. Existing Remove Ads purchases also include Premium; use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account.

Voice transcription requires on-device Speech support for the selected language.

Requires macOS 12 or later. Native desktop and Notification Center widgets require macOS 14 or later.

Interface languages: English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
