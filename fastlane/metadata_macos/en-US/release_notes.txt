Onlynote is now available as a native Mac app.
• Write and organize notes with native menus, Command-key shortcuts and right-click actions.
• Add paper widgets to your desktop or Notification Center on macOS 14 or later.
• Import photos, recognize text on-device, and use Premium voice memos.
• Save and restore .onlynote backups with a Mac Save As dialog.
• The Mac app is ad-free and supports restoring your existing Premium purchase.

Requires macOS 12 or later. Each device keeps its own local library; transfer notes and media through backups.
