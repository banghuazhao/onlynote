Onlynote is a notes and to-do list app for shopping lists, daily tasks and ideas you want to keep. No account needed.

Write and plan
Create notes, build checklists and add photos. Check off items as you go, with completed items moving to the bottom of the list. Add a voice recording when speaking is easier than typing.

Keep useful details close
Add the home screen widget to see pinned and recent notes, then open a note or start a new one.

Find what you saved
Organise notes into folders and search by title or content. Pin important notes and drag cards into the order that works for you.

Make notes your own
Choose note colours, font styles, text size and card size for a library that suits the way you read.

Remember and share
Set a reminder for a note. Share a note as an image when you want to send a list or an idea.

Recover a deleted note
Open Trash to restore a note you removed, or delete it permanently.

Use Onlynote for quick memos, shopping lists, daily tasks and notes with photos.
